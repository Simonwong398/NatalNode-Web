## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account information

If you create or use an account, we may process information such as:

- email address;
- authentication identifiers;
- account status;
- authentication and security records;
- account preferences and settings.

Where third-party sign-in is offered, we receive only the information necessary
to authenticate or operate the account as permitted by that provider and your
settings.

### 1.2 Birth and chart information

To provide chart-related features, you may provide information including:

- birth date;
- birth time, where known;
- birthplace or location query;
- selected canonical birthplace;
- geographic coordinates derived from the selected location;
- IANA timezone identity;
- time-resolution information required to establish the relevant instant;
- chart configuration choices;
- resulting chart data and related interpretations.

NatalNode treats birth and chart information as privacy-sensitive product
information even where a particular law does not classify every component as
legally sensitive personal information.

If birth time is unknown, NatalNode may preserve that state as unknown rather
than inventing a substitute time.

### 1.3 Saved charts and Vault information

If you choose to save charts or related material to a Vault or similar account
feature, we may store:

- chart inputs;
- chart results;
- labels or names you assign;
- associated notes or settings;
- timestamps and account relationships necessary to operate the saved feature.

Saving information is separate from merely entering information for a
calculation where the product architecture permits transient use.

### 1.4 Information about other people

The Service may allow you to enter information relating to another person, for
example when creating or comparing charts.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have an appropriate lawful basis or
permission to provide another person’s personal information to NatalNode.

You should not submit information about another person when you do not have a
legitimate reason or appropriate authority to do so.

### 1.5 Community, practitioner, feedback, and other submissions

Where these features are available, we may process information you voluntarily
submit through:

- community questions or discussions;
- practitioner profiles or applications;
- moderation processes;
- feedback forms;
- support communications;
- reports or other user-generated submissions.

Public submissions may be visible to other users as indicated by the relevant
feature.

### 1.6 Technical and security information

When you use the Service, servers and security systems may automatically
process information such as:

- IP address;
- request timestamps;
- browser and device information;
- operating-system information;
- requested routes;
- session and authentication events;
- security events;
- diagnostic and error information;
- coarse network or regional information when required for security or service
  delivery.

NatalNode does not require precise device geolocation merely to infer your
birthplace. Birthplace authority is established through explicit location input
and candidate selection.

### 1.7 Cookies and similar technologies

NatalNode may use cookies or equivalent local technologies that are necessary
to:

- maintain authentication sessions;
- preserve security;
- prevent abuse;
- remember essential preferences;
- provide core Service functionality.

Non-essential analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking technologies will
not be introduced without the disclosures and controls required by applicable
law.

## 2. How We Use Personal Information

We may process personal information to:

- provide requested charting and related Service functionality;
- resolve user-entered places into canonical locations;
- establish the time information required for requested chart calculations;
- create and manage accounts;
- operate saved-chart and Vault functionality;
- provide community, practitioner, feedback, or support features;
- authenticate users and maintain sessions;
- secure the Service and detect fraud, abuse, attacks, or technical failures;
- diagnose and improve reliability, accessibility, and performance;
- communicate with you about your account or the Service;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms;
- protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users, NatalNode, and others.

NatalNode does not use personal birth information to fabricate astronomical
facts when authoritative calculation inputs or services are unavailable.

## 3. Legal Bases for Processing

Where laws such as the GDPR require a legal basis, NatalNode relies as
appropriate on:

Performance of a contract or steps requested by you — for example, providing
an account, generating a requested chart, resolving a requested location, or
maintaining content you choose to save.

Legitimate interests — for example, operating and securing the Service,
detecting abuse, debugging failures, maintaining network and information
security, and improving reliability where those interests are not overridden by
your rights.

Consent — where consent is legally required, including for optional
communications or non-essential technologies.

Legal obligations — where processing is necessary to comply with applicable
law, valid legal process, accounting requirements, or regulatory duties.

## 4. Location Resolution and GeoNames

NatalNode may use GeoNames or another approved location-information provider to
help convert place searches into structured location candidates.

Location-resolution information may include:

- the place query you submit;
- canonical place name;
- country and administrative-region information;
- latitude and longitude;
- timezone identity;
- provider record identifiers.

NatalNode requires explicit selection where multiple plausible locations exist
and does not treat raw user text as authoritative location identity.

GeoNames data and web services are provided under an attribution license and
GeoNames describes its data as provided “as is.” NatalNode therefore maintains
provider attribution where applicable.

A location provider establishes place identity and related geographic metadata.
It does not by itself establish authoritative historical birth-time offsets or
astronomical chart facts.

NatalNode may use authorized third-party infrastructure, data, communications,
hosting, database, security, email, and astronomy-related providers where

### Other users

Information is disclosed to other users only when you intentionally use a
public or collaborative feature, such as public community content or a public

### Legal and safety disclosures

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to:

- comply with applicable law or valid legal process;
- respond to lawful government requests;
- investigate fraud or abuse;
- protect the security of the Service;
- protect legal rights or safety.

### Business transfers

acquired, merged, transferred, or sold, personal information may be transferred
as part of that transaction subject to applicable law and appropriate
protections.

If the Service moves from an individual founder-held development structure to a
later operating entity, required notices and controller information will be
updated before or as required by that transition.

## 8. Data Retention

NatalNode retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary

- operating an active account;
- maintaining information you have chosen to save;
- providing requested services;
- maintaining security and abuse-prevention records;

- enforcing agreements;

deleted or de-identified according to the applicable deletion process, subject

retention requirements.

rotation completes.

NatalNode will maintain an internal retention schedule appropriate to the
production architecture rather than retain information indefinitely without
purpose.
