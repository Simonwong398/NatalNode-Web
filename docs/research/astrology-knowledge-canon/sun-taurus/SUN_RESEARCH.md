# Sun Research

Status: RESEARCH_ONLY

Research package: WEB-P2-ASTROLOGY-KNOWLEDGE-RESEARCH-001

This dossier separates historical significations, modern reference language,
NatalNode policy, and editorial synthesis. It does not assert that astrology
is a scientific personality model. It does not change the interpretation
runtime.

## 1. Scope and object boundary

The object under review is the astrological Sun in a Western tropical natal
chart. The astronomical chart fact, zodiac model, and calculated placement
belong to the Engine/chart-fact lane. This dossier researches symbolic language
that may later explain that fact.

The package uses three distinct lenses:

1. Classical/traditional Western astrology, represented here by Ptolemy and
   Lilly.
2. Early-modern and modernizing sign-based astrology, represented here by Alan
   Leo.
3. Modern Western and psychological reference language, represented here by
   Astro.com AstroWiki.

These lenses are compared, not merged into a single timeless doctrine.

## 3. Historical and traditional findings

### 3.1 Lilly's social and solar layer

The Lilly-attributed material gives the Sun a traditional field that includes
authority, kings or magistrates, honour, preferment, and related social
significations. It also includes historical bodily and temperamental
correspondences. These are evidence of what that tradition signified, not
evidence that a modern user has a fixed social role, gender, body type, or
career.

For NatalNode, the safe result is:

- authority and honour can be shown as historical traditional vocabulary;
- they are not the default modern meaning of identity;
- bodily, medical, gendered, and status claims remain history/comparison-only;
- a historical attribution must be visible whenever the language is taught.

### 3.2 What the traditional source does not establish

The historical material does not establish that all Western astrologers have
always used the Sun as psychological identity, nor that a Sun sign alone
describes a person. The traditional system also contains technical conditions
that are not reproduced by a short sign-only explanation.

The historical layer therefore supports a school comparison, not a universal
Sun definition.

## 4. Early-modern and modernizing findings

Alan Leo's Sun-in-Taurus delineation is useful evidence for the development of
character-oriented, sign-based language. The passage associates the placement
with greater firmness, endurance, determination, and a slower, more cautious,
quieter, and practical tendency in that author's framework.

This is a direct historical finding about Leo's text. It is not admitted as a
NatalNode personality rule because:

- it belongs to a named historical school;
- the wording is broad and can sound deterministic when detached from its
  source;
- it does not account for house, aspects, angularity, the rest of the chart,
  lived context, or competing evidence;
- current content must paraphrase rather than reproduce the historical prose.

The appropriate content state is
SOURCE_SUPPORTED_WITH_CONDITIONS: historical school comparison, not default
personal interpretation.

## 5. Modern and psychological reference findings

AstroWiki's modern reference language gives the Sun a central role in life
force and personality, and relates it to identity, self-development,
integration, and active or creative expression. The same reference boundary
matters as much as the positive vocabulary: the Sun is not a sufficient basis
for an entire horoscope.

This supports a modern-lens policy candidate:

Sun may be explained as a central organizing or self-development function
within a named modern Western lens, provided that the explanation states that
the sign is one layer of a wider chart and does not convert the symbolism into
scientific prediction.

The phrase identity is therefore not rejected, but it is not made a
cross-tradition universal. It remains a modern-school or product-lens term.

## 6. School comparison

| Lens | Sun emphasis evidenced here | What it does not license |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classical/traditional | Solar function, authority, honour, office, vitality, and historically gendered or bodily significations | A universal psychological identity definition or a personal status prediction |
| Early-modern / modernizing | Character tendencies and sign-based individual description, including the historical Sun-in-Taurus passage | Treating one historical delineation as a complete chart reading |
| Modern / psychological | Life force, central personality, identity, self-development, integration, active and creative expression | Claiming that the Sun alone explains the whole person or proves a scientific trait |

The differences are real but not absolute. All three lenses give the Sun a
central or organizing importance in their own terms; they disagree on what
that importance means and how personal or technical the claim should be.

## 7. Proposed NatalNode Sun policy

This is a recommendation for Product Owner admission, not a silent policy
change.

| Sun language | Proposed policy state | Eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Life force or vitality | ADOPTED_WITH_CONDITIONS | Modern or comparative explanation, with non-scientific disclosure |
| Central organizing function / self-development | ADOPTED_WITH_CONDITIONS | Modern/psychological lens only; whole-chart limit required |
| Identity or self-expression | ADOPTED_WITH_CONDITIONS | Named modern lens; never the only chart conclusion |
| Authority, honour, office | EDUCATIONAL_ONLY or SUPPORTED_AS_ALTERNATIVE | Traditional-history or named traditional comparison |
| Father, masculine, gendered role | EDUCATIONAL_ONLY | Historical comparison only unless separately admitted with a narrow policy |
| Medical, bodily, or status prediction | NOT_USED_FOR_PERSONAL_INTERPRETATION | No production personal wording |
| Purpose, destiny, or guaranteed life direction | UNRESOLVED / SOURCE_RESEARCH_REQUIRED | Requires separate evidence and policy |

The recommended default is plural and explicit: modern bounded language for
current reflective education, with historical traditional material in a
clearly labelled comparison layer.

## 8. Sun research state

The Sun primitive is source-complete enough for a Product Owner decision on
bounded vocabulary, but not for a universal production doctrine. The following
claims remain intentionally conditional:

- identity is a modern lens, not a timeless definition;
- authority and honour are historical/traditional alternatives;
- father and masculine symbolism are contested across use cases;
- purpose and destiny require additional research;
- all personal expressions require chart modifiers and a whole-chart limit.

No production interpretation runtime is admitted by this dossier.
