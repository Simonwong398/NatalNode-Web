## 1. Composition rule

The composition must preserve the authority of each input:

1. The Sun supplies a function or subject, with the chosen lens made explicit.
2. Taurus supplies a sign structure and vocabulary, with the chosen tradition
   made explicit.
3. The composition asks how the first layer may be explored through the second.
4. House, aspects, angularity, Venus/dispositor, dignity, degree, chart
   emphasis, birth-time authority, and lived context can modify the result.
5. The output must show at least one constructive possibility, one tension, one
   counter-condition, and one limit.

This rule prevents keyword concatenation such as Sun equals identity plus
Taurus equals stubborn. It also prevents a modern sign convention from being
presented as a classical universal.

## 3. Rationale by lens

### Modern and psychological composition

Under a named modern Western lens, the Sun may be explored as a central
organizing or self-development function expressed through Taurus's modern
Earth, Fixed, and Venus structural vocabulary. A bounded editorial synthesis
can therefore ask about continuity, tangible values, embodiment, and sustained
expression.

The words may, explored, and through matter. They indicate a reflective
question, not a personality verdict.

### Traditional and historical composition

The classical/traditional material should not be compressed into the modern
identity sentence. A historically labelled explanation may compare solar
authority or honour with Taurus's solid classification and historical
triplicity, but it must preserve the original technical scope. A later
traditional reading would require more than a sign-only formula.

### Early-modern composition

Alan Leo provides a historical example of a character-oriented Sun-in-Taurus
description. It can illustrate how one school translated the placement into
firmness, endurance, determination, caution, and practical pace. It must not
be silently promoted to NatalNode's default voice.

## 4. Source-supported synthesis candidate

Proposed bounded synthesis:

Under the modern Western lens used here, a solar function may be explored
through Taurus's emphasis on continuity, tangible or embodied values, and
sustained expression. The same pattern can support follow-through and
consolidation, while raising a question about whether attachment to established
conditions makes revision harder. House, aspects, Venus/dispositor, chart
emphasis, birth-time authority, and the rest of the chart are required before
the question can be made more specific.

## 5. Constructive possibilities

These are conditional questions, not traits:

- Where might a person sustain a valued direction long enough to make it
  tangible?
- Where might continuity, patience, or maintenance support the solar aim?
- Which values deserve embodiment rather than remaining abstract?
- What kind of visible or creative expression benefits from steady
  development?

These questions are eligible for a research projection only. They do not
establish calmness, wealth, talent, or success.

## 6. Tension possibilities

These are bounded questions, not diagnoses:

- When does persistence become reluctance to revise?
- When does preserving value become attachment to an established condition?
- Does a need for tangible confirmation narrow the available options?
- Could a slower consolidation rhythm conflict with a time-sensitive context?

The tension layer is CONTESTED for default personal use. It requires chart
modifiers and user context, and must not be rendered as stubbornness or
inflexibility.

## 8. Negative boundary

Sun in Taurus does not, by itself, mean that someone is:

- calm, rich, luxurious, materialistic, or slow;
- inevitably stubborn, possessive, resistant, or unable to change;
- loyal in every relationship or secure in every value;
- destined for a profession, status, result, or life path;
- fully described by a single placement;
- scientifically predicted by astrology.

The boundary is a required part of any future content package.

## 10. Barnum and quality-gate result

Generic phrases such as persistent, practical, loyal, or values-driven are
highly reusable across signs and people. They fail a Barnum check when stated
as facts without a source lens, modifier, counter-condition, and a question
that could be answered differently.

The package passes the structural checks for object separation, source
traceability, tradition labels, and negative boundaries. It does not pass the
final production quality gate because Product Owner policy decisions remain
unresolved and the tension vocabulary is not source-complete for personal
interpretation.

Quality result: REVISE.
