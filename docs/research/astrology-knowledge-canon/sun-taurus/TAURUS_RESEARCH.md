# Taurus Research

Status: RESEARCH_ONLY

Research package: WEB-P2-ASTROLOGY-KNOWLEDGE-RESEARCH-001

This dossier researches Taurus as a sign object in Western tropical astrology.
It distinguishes the classical term solid, the modern term Fixed, the
modern Earth convention, and Venus rulership. It does not treat any of these
as a scientific personality result.

## 1. Source-backed structural findings

### 1.1 Classical solid structure

In the cited chapter of Ptolemy's Tetrabiblos, Taurus is one of the four solid
signs, alongside Leo, Scorpio, and Aquarius. Ptolemy explains the classification
through the seasonal sequence and the way the seasonal conditions become more
firmly established after the solstitial or equinoctial signs.

This is a historical classification in a cosmological system. It is not
evidence that a person with Taurus prominent is stubborn, immovable, or unable
to change.

Source: [Ptolemy, Tetrabiblos, Book I chapter 11](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/thayer/e/roman/texts/ptolemy/tetrabiblos/1b%2A.html).

### 1.2 Historical triplicity context

Ptolemy groups Taurus, Virgo, and Capricorn as a triangle or triplicity and
describes a historical governance context involving Venus primarily and Saturn
jointly. This is valuable evidence for the history of Taurus within a
technical system, but it should not be flattened into the modern beginner
phrase Earth sign.

Source: [Ptolemy, Tetrabiblos, Book II chapter 3](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/thayer/e/roman/texts/ptolemy/tetrabiblos/2a%2A.html).

### 1.3 Modern Earth, Fixed, and Venus convention

The modern reference consulted for this package labels Taurus Earth, Fixed,
and Venus-ruled. This gives NatalNode a usable modern structural vocabulary,
provided that the source lens is named and the labels are not presented as
physical or psychological facts.

Source: [Astro.com AstroWiki: Taurus](https://www.astro.com/astrowiki/en/Taurus).

## 2. Earth research result

Earth is useful in a beginner-facing explanation when it is presented as a
symbolic family of questions about tangible experience, embodiment, material
conditions, practical attention, and stability. The family is broader than
practicality and does not imply that a person is wealthy, materialistic,
ungenerous, or uninterested in imagination.

The status is SOURCE_SUPPORTED_WITH_CONDITIONS:

- the Earth label is a modern structural convention;
- the associated vocabulary is editorial synthesis from the modern reference;
- no one adjective is a deterministic result;
- other chart layers can reinforce, redirect, or contradict the emphasis.

## 3. Fixed and solid research result

The relationship between Ptolemy's solid and the modern Fixed category is a
historical-to-modern bridge, not an identity claim. NatalNode may explain that
the modern Fixed vocabulary is related to continuation, consolidation,
maintenance, or sustained emphasis, while stating that the terminology and
doctrine are not unchanged from Ptolemy.

The safe bridge is:

solid seasonal consolidation
to a modern question about how a sign may maintain, stabilize, or continue an
emphasis.

The unsafe shortcut is:

Fixed
equals stubborn
equals permanent personality.

Persistence, consistency, resistance to revision, and inertia may be
conditional constructive or tension questions, but they require the chosen
method, the rest of the chart, and the user's context.

Sources: [Ptolemy Book I chapter 11](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/thayer/e/roman/texts/ptolemy/tetrabiblos/1b%2A.html) and
[AstroWiki Taurus](https://www.astro.com/astrowiki/en/Taurus).

## 4. Venus rulership research result

The Lilly-attributed traditional material gives Venus rulership of Taurus in
the sign-rulership scheme and preserves additional dignity and method details.
The modern reference also presents Taurus as Venus-ruled. These are two
different kinds of support:

- the traditional layer is a named historical rulership system;
- the modern layer is a current Western reference convention;
- a rulership relation is not itself a complete personality interpretation;
- a later dispositor or dignity reading requires its own technical inputs.

NatalNode may teach Venus rulership as a structural relation. It must not
silently turn it into romance, luxury, money, taste, or relationship
predictions.

Sources: [Lilly Venus access page](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/venus_att.html) and
[AstroWiki Taurus](https://www.astro.com/astrowiki/en/Taurus).

## 5. Early-modern Taurus vocabulary

Alan Leo's historical Sun-in-Taurus passage supplies a relevant sign-based
example: in that school, the solar expression is described through firmness,
endurance, determination, and a slower, more cautious, quieter, practical
tendency. This is evidence of a historical style of delineation rather than a
source-complete definition of Taurus.

Source: [Alan Leo, Astrology for All scan](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1899__leo___astrology_for_all.pdf).

NatalNode should paraphrase this material, preserve the school attribution,
and avoid treating it as a universal personal label.
