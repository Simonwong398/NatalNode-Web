Direct means the source states or classifies the claim in the named scope.
Inferred means the claim is a bounded bridge or composition made from source
inputs. Agreement refers to the evidence in this package, not to universal
agreement among all astrology schools.

## Matrix result

The research closes the initial P0 evidence gap for the named sources and
creates a reviewable claim record. It does not turn every supported historical
or modern statement into NatalNode doctrine. Conditional, contested, and
rejected rows remain visible so that localization or generation cannot silently
strengthen them.

The package is therefore RESEARCH_COMPLETE_WITH_UNRESOLVED_POLICY. A later
Product Owner admission task must choose the default lens, approve the bounded
modern structural vocabulary, and decide which historical material remains
educational-only before any production interpretation content is authorized.
