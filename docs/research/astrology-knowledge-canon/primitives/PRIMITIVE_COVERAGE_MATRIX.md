## Coverage interpretation

- QUICK, PLAIN, and LEARNER are bounded research templates, not admitted
  product copy, for all objects except the preserved Sun/Taurus package.
- ASTROLOGER and TECHNICAL depth deliberately remain incomplete. This avoids
  pretending that a beginner vocabulary is a professional or technical
  doctrine.
- The matrix does not authorize a Planet × Sign matrix, a runtime import, or
  localization. Those are separate policy and acceptance decisions.
