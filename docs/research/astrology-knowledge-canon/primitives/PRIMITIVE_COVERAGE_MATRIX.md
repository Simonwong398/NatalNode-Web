template may be prepared for editorial review. Neither status means production
content is complete or runtime is enabled.

## Coverage interpretation

- QUICK, PLAIN, and LEARNER are now editorial-package candidates for the
  admitted semantic layer; they are not automatically production content.
- ASTROLOGER and TECHNICAL depth deliberately remain incomplete. This avoids
  pretending that a beginner vocabulary is a professional or technical
  doctrine.
- Scorpio, Aquarius, and Pisces are admitted only at the structural layer;
  deeper psychological vocabulary remains SOURCE_RESEARCH_REQUIRED.
- The matrix does not authorize a Planet × Sign matrix, a runtime import, or
  localization. Those are separate policy and acceptance decisions.
