## Review and citation rules

1. A source page is not an automatic admission of every statement associated
   with its subject.
2. Indexed excerpts support only the bounded claim recorded in the matching
   research file; no inaccessible page is described as fully reviewed.
3. Historical, medical, gendered, predictive, and deterministic statements
   remain separately tagged even when a source states them directly.
4. Any claim marked SOURCE_RESEARCH_REQUIRED is visible as a gap and cannot be
   promoted by localization, generation, or runtime code.
5. The source corpus supports a reviewable primitive package. It does not
   settle advanced dignity, dispositor, mutual-reception, phase, transit, or
   provider questions.
