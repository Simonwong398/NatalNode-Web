# Elements — Primitive Research Package V1

Objects: Fire, Earth, Air, Water
Source status: SOURCE_SUPPORTED_WITH_CONDITIONS for the bounded structural
model.
Policy status: ADOPTED_WITH_CONDITIONS; Earth remains reconciled with the
already admitted Taurus policy, and the standalone element policy is now
admitted with the conditions below.
Runtime: RUNTIME_NOT_ADMITTED

The elements are treated as a structural vocabulary for what kind of field,
quality, or mode a sign is conventionally said to emphasize. They are not
temperament tests, diagnoses, personality types, or deterministic outcomes.
The Western tropical model is used here; non-Western elemental systems are out
of scope.

## Negative boundary

An element cannot by itself establish emotion, intelligence, sociability,
wealth, health, morality, career, relationship behavior, or destiny. This
boundary is SOURCE_SUPPORTED as NatalNode policy and ADOPTED as a product rule.
The standalone element records remain RUNTIME_NOT_ADMITTED.
