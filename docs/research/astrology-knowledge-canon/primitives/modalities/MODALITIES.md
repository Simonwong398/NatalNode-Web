# Modalities — Primitive Research Package V1

Objects: Cardinal, Fixed, Mutable
Source status: SOURCE_SUPPORTED_WITH_CONDITIONS for the bounded structural
model.
Policy status: READY_FOR_OWNER_ADMISSION; Fixed remains reconciled with the
already admitted Taurus policy, but standalone modality policy is not yet
admitted.
Runtime: RUNTIME_NOT_ADMITTED

Modalities describe a conventional operating mode or phase of sign expression.
They are not personality categories. Modern Cardinal/Fixed/Mutable language is
bridged to historical seasonal, solid, and bicorporeal descriptions only when
the layer is named.

## Element × modality model

The package admits a compositional rule for future research only:

> The element supplies a broad field or quality; the modality supplies an
> operating mode within that field. Neither component is a personality verdict,
> and their combination is not a full interpretation.

This is claim MODE-COMP-001, sourced as a bounded synthesis from PC-01, PC-11,
PC-12, and PC-19. Its claim status is SOURCE_SUPPORTED_WITH_CONDITIONS; its
policy status is UNRESOLVED.

The model is deliberately not a 12-cell interpretation matrix. The existing
Taurus package may continue to use Earth plus Fixed with its already admitted
conditions. Other combinations require a separate Product Owner policy
package, source review, and modifier rules.

## Historical and negative boundary

Classical seasonal, solid, and bicorporeal descriptions remain EDUCATIONAL_ONLY.
They are claim status SOURCE_SUPPORTED_WITH_CONDITIONS where directly sourced,
but must not be silently merged with modern personality language. The rules
that a modality alone proves temperament, reliability, dominance, or flexibility
are REJECTED and NOT_USED_FOR_PERSONAL_INTERPRETATION.
