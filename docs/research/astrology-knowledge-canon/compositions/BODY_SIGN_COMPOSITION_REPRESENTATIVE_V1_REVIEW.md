## 2. Frozen method

```text
BODY FUNCTION
  -> SIGN CONDITIONS
  -> STRUCTURAL INTERACTION
  -> BOUNDED CHART QUESTION
  -> CONSTRUCTIVE POTENTIAL / TRADE-OFF
  -> MODIFIERS REQUIRED
```

The body is the function being explored. The sign is the structural context.
Element supplies a broad field; modality supplies a mode within that field;
rulership supplies a named sign-to-planet relation. Element, modality, and
rulership are derived sign layers and do not create independent evidence.

Each record has exactly **one** independent evidence unit: the authoritative
`BODY_SIGN_PLACEMENT` fact. Body and sign remain distinct semantic dimensions
of that same placement because they answer different questions. A later
convergence may use a genuinely different authoritative placement fact, such
as Moon in Aries plus Mercury in Aries, or a separately authoritative admitted
aspect. Repeating Moon in Aries cannot self-converge.

Aries is not Mars. Taurus is not Venus. Fixed is not Taurus. Moon is not Water.
Mercury is not Air. Those collapse tests fail the method deliberately.

## 3. Representative composition dossiers

### 3.1 Moon × Aries

**Composition ID:** `body.moon_x_sign.aries`

**BODY INPUT**

Moon supplies questions about response, recurring rhythm, receptivity, inner
experience, and needs. This is a symbolic function, not an emotions-only
definition or a whole-person result.

**SIGN INPUT + ELEMENT/MODALITY/RULERSHIP**

Aries supplies a sign context for initiation, activation, assertion, and
movement into a new phase. Fire supplies a broad activation field. Cardinal
supplies the operating mode of beginning or forming. Traditional Mars is a
named Aries relation in the seven-visible-planet baseline; it is not the whole
meaning of the sign and does not replace the Moon function.

**INTEGRATED COMPOSITION**

A Moon function may be explored through Aries structure as a question about how
recurring response, receptivity, or need meets initiation, activation, or
assertion: what is received may be invited to take an initial form without
turning that interaction into a fixed emotional style.

**BOUNDED CHART QUESTION**

When an authoritative chart fact places the Moon in Aries, what recurring
response or need is being asked to take an initial form, and what context would
show whether beginning or receptivity is more relevant?

**CONSTRUCTIVE POTENTIAL**

Notice what helps a recurring need or response move from reception into a
first, consciously observed action while keeping the response visible and the
initiating context open to review.

**TRADE-OFF**

A need for receptivity or familiar rhythm may meet a context that asks for
initiation. The useful tension is how to begin without erasing the response
that still needs to be heard.

**FORBIDDEN INFERENCES**

This does not entail emotionally impulsive, angry, impatient, aggressive,
emotionally unstable, or excitement-seeking behaviour. It does not entail a
complete emotional identity, personality, or outcome. It does not turn Aries
into Mars.

**MODIFIERS**

The placement fact and Western Tropical model are required. School choice is
required for a narrower reading. House, house system, aspects, orb, phase,
degrees, other chart factors, counterevidence, and lived context are either
conditional or required before they are used. Unknown birth time leaves an
authoritative body × sign fact available but blocks ASC, MC, houses, and other

**NAIVE KEYWORD CONCATENATION**

`Moon = emotions + Aries = impulsive + Fire = energy + Cardinal = action + Mars = aggression`

**BOUNDED COMPOSITION**

`Moon response/need function × Aries initiation conditions` asks how a recurring
response or need may be invited to take an initial form, then keeps the
response, context, and possible revision visible.

## 5. Double-counting and rulership audit

### 5.1 Primitive collapse tests

The following substitutions are invalid and are explicitly rejected:

| Invalid collapse | Why it fails |
| --- | --- |
| Aries = Mars | A sign and its ruler are different object classes; rulership is a named relation. |
| Taurus = Venus | A sign and its ruler are different object classes; the ruler's full body function is not imported. |
| Fixed = Taurus | Fixed is shared by Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, and Aquarius. |
| Moon = Water | Moon is a body function; Water is an element grouping for signs. |
| Mercury = Air | Mercury is a body function; Air is an element grouping for signs. |

### 5.2 Evidence accounting

Each record has exactly one independent placement-level unit, represented by
`placementEvidence.evidenceType: BODY_SIGN_PLACEMENT` and
`placementEvidence.independentEvidenceUnits: 1`. Its body and sign are
semantic dimensions of that one fact, not two evidence units. Fire/Earth,
Cardinal/Fixed, and Mars/Venus are derived explanatory layers. They clarify the
sign but cannot create convergence or inflate a later Signal's confidence,
strength, or priority. Repeated references to the same placement also cannot
increase confidence or priority.

### 5.3 Rulership boundary

The traditional seven-visible-planet baseline is visible in every record. The
record includes `importsCompleteRulerSemantics: false` and
`independentEvidence: false`. Modern outer-planet alternatives are not
applicable to Aries or Taurus in the basic mapping; no co-ruler is silently
added. Advanced dignity, dispositor, mutual reception, triplicity, bounds,
faces, exaltation, fall, and detriment remain out of scope.
